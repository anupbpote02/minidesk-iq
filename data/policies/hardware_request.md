# Hardware Request Policy

**Document owner:** IT Asset Management
**Last updated:** 2026-01-20
**Applies to:** All employees and long-term contractors

## Overview

This policy covers how employees request new or replacement hardware (laptops,
monitors, peripherals, and mobile devices), approval thresholds, and standard
fulfillment timelines.

## Standard Issue Equipment

Every full-time employee is issued the following at onboarding, at no additional
approval required:

- One laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or Dell Latitude, based on role/team standard)
- One external monitor (24" or 27")
- Keyboard, mouse, and laptop dock
- Standard noise-canceling headset for roles involving frequent calls

## Requesting Replacement Hardware

Submit a "Hardware Request" ticket through MiniDesk IQ with:

- The item needed and reason (damaged, lost, stolen, upgrade, or new hire).
- Asset tag of the item being replaced, if applicable.
- Manager name for approval routing.

### Fulfillment Timelines

| Request Type              | Approval Needed        | Typical Fulfillment |
|----------------------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Damaged equipment (defect)| None (auto-approved)   | 2-3 business days   |
| Lost or stolen equipment  | Manager approval        | 3-5 business days   |
| Upgrade request           | Manager + IT budget review | 5-10 business days |
| New hire standard kit     | None (auto-approved)   | Ready by start date |
| Non-standard equipment    | Manager + Department head | 10-15 business days |

## Loss or Theft

Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours so IT Security can
remotely lock and wipe the device. Report via MiniDesk IQ with category
"Hardware Request" and mention "lost/stolen" in the description — this also
triggers an automatic Security notification.

## Approval Thresholds

- Requests under $500 in replacement value: manager approval only.
- Requests $500–$2,000: manager + IT budget owner approval.
- Requests over $2,000 (e.g., specialized workstations, high-end monitors for
  design/engineering roles): manager, IT budget owner, and department head
  approval required.

## Loaner Equipment

While a replacement is being fulfilled, employees may request a loaner laptop
or monitor from the local IT closet at each office. Loaners are limited to a
30-day checkout period.

## Returning Equipment

All company-issued hardware must be returned within 5 business days of an
employee's last day, or when upgrading to new equipment. Unreturned equipment
valued over $200 may be deducted from final pay where legally permitted, in
accordance with local labor law.
