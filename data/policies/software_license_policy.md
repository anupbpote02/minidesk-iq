# Software License Request Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines how employees can request, install, and manage licensed software on company-owned devices.

## Who Can Request Software
All full-time and contract employees may request software needed to perform their job duties. Requests must be submitted through the IT service desk.

## Approved Software Categories
- Productivity tools (e.g., Microsoft Office, Google Workspace add-ons)
- Development tools (e.g., IDEs, version control clients)
- Design tools (e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma)
- Communication tools (e.g., Slack, Zoom paid tiers)

Software outside these categories requires manager approval and a brief business justification.

## How to Request a License
1. Submit a ticket via the IT service desk describing the software name, version, and business purpose.
2. IT reviews the request within 2 business days.
3. If approved, IT will either provision an existing license seat or purchase a new one, depending on availability.
4. The employee receives installation instructions or an activation key via their company email.

## Cost Thresholds
- Under $50/year per seat: auto-approved if the software is on the approved list.
- $50–$500/year per seat: requires direct manager approval.
- Over $500/year per seat: requires department head approval and a procurement review.

## License Renewal
Software licenses are reviewed annually. Employees will receive a notification 30 days before a license expires and must confirm continued need, or the seat will be reclaimed and reassigned.

## Unauthorized Software
Installing unlicensed or pirated software on company devices is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action. Employees who discover unauthorized software on their device should report it to IT immediately.

## Offboarding
When an employee leaves the company, all software licenses assigned to them are automatically revoked and reassigned to the license pool within 24 hours of their last working day.

## Contact
For questions about this policy or to check the status of a request, contact the IT service desk or ask MiniDesk IQ directly (e.g., "What's the status of my software request?").
