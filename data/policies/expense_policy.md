# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document owner:** Finance Department
**Last updated:** 2026-01-05
**Applies to:** All employees

## Overview

This policy explains what business expenses are reimbursable, how to submit
expense reports, and the approval and reimbursement timeline.

## Reimbursable Expense Categories

- Business travel (flights, trains, rideshare, mileage at the current IRS/company rate)
- Client meals and team meals during approved offsites (up to $75/person/day)
- Home office setup stipend (see Remote Work Policy), one-time $500
- Professional development: courses, certifications, and conference tickets
  up to $1,500/year with manager approval
- Software or SaaS tools required for the role and not already provided centrally,
  up to $200/month without further approval

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Personal entertainment unrelated to business purpose
- Alcohol at non-client events (client-facing meals may include reasonable
  alcohol per local norms, subject to manager discretion)
- Traffic or parking fines
- First-class or business-class flights for trips under 6 hours, unless
  medically required with documentation on file with HR

## How to Submit an Expense Report

1. Log expenses in the Finance expense system within 30 days of the purchase.
2. Attach an itemized receipt for any expense over $25.
3. Select the correct cost center and category (e.g., "Travel," "Professional
   Development," "Home Office Stipend").
4. Submit for manager approval.

## Approval Thresholds

| Expense Amount        | Approval Required                     |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Under $200             | Manager approval only                 |
| $200 - $1,000           | Manager + Finance review              |
| Over $1,000             | Manager + Finance + Department head   |

## Reimbursement Timeline

- Approved expense reports are reimbursed via direct deposit within the next
  two payroll cycles (typically 5-10 business days after approval).
- Corporate card charges are reconciled automatically and do not require a
  separate reimbursement, but still require timely categorization and receipts.

## Corporate Card Program

- Employees who travel frequently (4+ trips/quarter) or manage vendor
  relationships are eligible for a corporate card upon manager request.
- Corporate card statements must be reconciled in the expense system monthly;
  unreconciled charges after 60 days may result in card suspension.

## Mileage Reimbursement

- Personal vehicle use for business purposes is reimbursed at the current
  standard mileage rate published annually by Finance (updated each January).
- Commuting between home and your regular office is not reimbursable.
