# Remote Work Policy

**Document owner:** People Operations & IT
**Last updated:** 2026-02-10
**Applies to:** All employees eligible for remote or hybrid work arrangements

## Overview

This policy defines eligibility, expectations, and technical requirements for
employees working remotely, whether full-time remote, hybrid, or occasional
work-from-home.

## Eligibility

- Roles are classified as **Remote**, **Hybrid** (minimum 2 office days/week),
  or **On-site** by department leadership during role creation.
- Employees in Hybrid or On-site roles may work from home occasionally with
  manager approval; no formal request is needed for fewer than 5 days/month.
- Fully remote arrangements for normally On-site or Hybrid roles require
  written approval from both the manager and HR Business Partner.

## Technical Requirements for Remote Work

- A stable internet connection with a minimum of 25 Mbps download / 5 Mbps
  upload is required.
- Company VPN (see VPN Access Policy) must be connected whenever accessing
  internal systems, customer data, or internal file shares.
- Work must be performed on a company-issued device. Personal devices may only
  be used for email and calendar via the approved mobile management (MDM)
  profile.
- Employees must enable disk encryption and auto-lock (5 minutes idle) on all
  company devices, which is enforced automatically via MDM.

## Working Hours & Availability

- Remote employees are expected to be reachable via Slack and calendar during
  their team's designated core hours (typically 10am-3pm local team time).
- Time zone differences of more than 3 hours from the team's primary location
  must be disclosed and agreed upon with the manager in advance.

## Home Office Stipend

- Fully remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend
  during their first 90 days, submitted via the Expense Reimbursement process.
- An additional $50/month stipend is provided for internet and utilities,
  paid automatically through payroll (no expense report needed).

## International Remote Work

- Working from a country other than your employment country for more than
  15 consecutive days requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax
  and labor law implications.
- Unapproved international remote work may affect payroll tax withholding
  and benefits eligibility.

## Security Expectations

- Do not work from unsecured public Wi-Fi without the VPN active.
- Lock your screen whenever stepping away from your device, even at home.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately per the Hardware Request Policy.
